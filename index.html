FOLLOW THE VALUE CHAIN

AI is a stack. Nobody builds it alone.

01 / COMPUTE

Start with the physical world

Chip equipment enables fabrication. Foundries manufacture chips. Designers turn those chips into accelerators for AI workloads.

02 / INTELLIGENCE

Compute becomes capability

Cloud providers operate compute infrastructure. Model labs use it to train and serve systems that generate text, images, and code.

03 / PRODUCTS

Capability finds a customer

Data platforms and applications connect models to useful work. Money, distribution, and feedback flow back through the stack.

A map, with its working notes attached.

Company values refer to the whole company, not its AI business. Market capitalization is the stock-market value of public equity; a private funding valuation is a negotiated snapshot. Neither measures revenue or AI leadership.

Public valuations are intentionally empty until a dated market-data source can be verified. Private figures are seed research from historical funding announcements, not current values. All relationships need source verification; arrows show the stated direction, not exclusivity or the complete industry. Companies may operate in several layers; the map shows one primary position.

The editable dataset lives in data/landscape.json. Each company has a valuation record, categories, and references. Each connection has a type, direction, description, date, and reference URL. No live market feed is connected.
